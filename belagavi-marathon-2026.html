The Proposition · Executive Summary

The route is the campaign

Turning Belagavi's living heritage into an active guided tour at pace, paired with a permanent digital transformation for district tourism.

01 8 km Course

Heritage at Marathon Pace

  • 6 Historic Landmarks in 8 km: Course deliberately connects Belagavi Fort, ancient Basadis, Safa Masjid, Postman Circle, and Chennamma memorial.
  • One Morning, City-Wide Focus: Closed roads direct all civic attention, 500 runners, and regional cameras at Belagavi's signature monuments.
  • Guided Tour at Pace: Replaces anonymous highway stretches with a continuous corridor of historic discovery.
02 Live Performances

Culture Staged at the Monuments

  • Authentic Folk Corridors: Yakshagana, Dollu Kunitha, Karadi Majalu & Veeragase troupes staged directly at landmark sites.
  • Beyond Plain Water Stops: Cultural points double as high-energy cheering zones and high-visibility media photo spots.
  • Dhol-Tasha Reception: High-octane finish arch reception welcoming every finisher at Kumar Gandharv Auditorium.
03 Theme Alignment · Mandatory

AI Tourism Website Revamp

  • Complete Tourism Portal Rebuild: Redesigning Belagavi's official tourism platform with cutting-edge AI features and interactive maps.
  • Official Inauguration at Function: Formal digital portal launch on stage by the Deputy Commissioner & District In-charge Minister.
  • AI Smart Tourist Guide: Interactive itineraries, digital guides, and multi-lingual voice assistants for lasting tourism impact.
04 Public Mobilization

17-Day Sprint to 500 Runners

  • Run Club Network: Direct engagement across Belagavi, Bengaluru, Hubballi, Dharwad, and Maharashtra borders.

Answering the Global Theme

Digital enhancement & artificial intelligence in tourism

The World Tourism Day theme is not just decoration. Belagavi's entire district tourism website is being completely rebuilt and enhanced with AI capabilities, inaugurated on event day.

01 · AI Smart Itinerary Engine

Interactive planning tool that customises Belagavi heritage trails based on visitor duration, interest (history, waterfalls, spiritual, cuisine), and real-time travel logistics.

02 · Digital Tourist Mitra (AI Assistant)

A multi-lingual conversational AI guide in Kannada, Marathi, and English providing instant historical context, audio commentary, and navigational guidance at every monument.

03 · Interactive Heritage Geotagging

High-resolution drone mapping, 3D monument walkthroughs of Belagavi Fort, Kamal Basti, and surrounding district treasures preserving Belagavi’s legacy online.

Inauguration Moment
The rebuilt AI Tourism Website will be formally unveiled on stage during Session 1 at Kumar Gandharv Auditorium.

Mobilization Strategy

How we reach 500+ runners in 17 days

A multi-channel institutional, school, and digital sprint to pack the start line and maximize community participation.

Channel 01 · School Visits Direct Engagement

School Assembly Talks & Pamphlet Distribution

  • Direct School Visits: Organizing on-ground visits to schools and colleges across Belagavi and surrounding talukas.
  • Assembly Presentations: Briefing students and faculty during morning school assemblies to encourage mass participation in the 8 km run.
  • Pamphlet Drive: Handing out registration flyers, parent consent forms, and competition details directly to youth.
Channel 02 · DC Directives Government Push

Letters to Officials & Government Schools

  • Official DC Circulars: Formal letters dispatched to all government schools, PU colleges, and ITIs to send student runners.
  • Departmental Mobilization: Directives to District Police, Sports Authority, NCC, NSS, and Scouts & Guides battalions.
  • Institutional Nominations: Designated teacher coordinators at each school managing student batches.
Channel 03 · Athlete Network Committed Runners

Run Clubs & Regional Sports Groups

  • City & Regional Run Clubs: Dedicated coordination with runner groups in Belagavi, Hubballi, Dharwad, Kolhapur & Bengaluru.
  • Athletic Associations: Engaging district track & field clubs for competitive category participation.
  • Bulk Club Registration: Priority registration batches and group bib allocations for running groups.
Channel 04 · Media Engine City-Wide Reach

Influencer Reels, Hoardings & Press

  • Regional Creators: Kannada and Marathi travel/fitness influencers producing route-reveal reels shot at Belagavi Fort.
  • Prime Outdoor Hoardings: High-visibility flex banners at Chennamma Circle, Fort, Central Bus Stand, and Lele Ground.
  • Print & Radio Press Note: Official DC press release issued to leading Kannada and Marathi daily newspapers.

Safety, Medical & Traffic

Hydration, First Aid & Route Marshals

  • Hydration Corridors: Water and electrolyte stations placed every 2 km along the course.
  • Advanced Medical Coverage: Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ALS) ambulances trailing the pack and stationed at Fort.
  • Volunteer Route Marshals: Trained volunteer marshals at every turn and major intersection.
  • Rolling Traffic Barricading: Rolling road closures coordinated seamlessly with Belagavi Traffic Police.
