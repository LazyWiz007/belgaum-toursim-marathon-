# Belagavi Marathon 2026 — World Tourism Day
**Event note for the Office of the Deputy Commissioner, Belagavi District**

- **Date:** Sunday, 27 September 2026 (World Tourism Day)
- **Theme:** Digital agenda and artificial intelligence to redesign tourism
- **Scale:** ~500 participants
- **Format:** 8 km heritage run through Belagavi's landmark tourist sites, followed by a formal function, prize distribution and cultural programme
- **Days to event:** 17 (as of 10 September 2026)

## 2. The route — 8 km heritage run

A single 8.8 km route. Every kilometre is deliberately routed past a tourist or heritage landmark, so the run itself is the tourism promotion.

| # | Point | Approx. km | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lele Ground (Subhash Chandra Ground)** — START | 0.0 | Assembly, bib collection, warm-up, flag-off |
| 2 | **Congress Well Park** | ~0.6 | Site linked to the 1924 Belgaum Congress Session presided over by Mahatma Gandhi |
| 2 | **Military Madhavr** | ~0.8 | 
| 3 | **World's First Postman Statue Circle** | ~3.0 | Belagavi's unique claim — the world's first statue honouring a postman |
| 4 | **Belagavi Fort area** (via Patil Galli) | ~5.0 | 13th-century fort; Kamal Basti, Safa Masjid, Jain temples within |
| 5 | **Kittur Rani Chennamma Circle** (via Khanapur Rd) | ~7.0 | Memorial to Rani Chennamma, among India's earliest anti-colonial leaders |
| 6 | **Finish —  auditorium** (via Jijamata Rd) | ~8.8 | Finish arch, refreshments, transition to function |


**Landmarks visible alongside the route** (for signage and commentary, not stops): Shri Kapileshwara Swamy Temple, Military Shri Mahadev Temple, Belagavi Bus Stand precinct, Khade Bazar.

> ⚠️ **Distances above are estimated from the map, not measured.** A ground recce with the traffic police is required to fix exact kilometre marks, barricading and closure timings.

> 📝 **Note on earlier draft:** the DC office's handwritten sheet proposed Belagavi Fort → Congress Bhavan (4 km) and Belagavi Fort → Chennamma Circle (1.5 km). The 8 km route supersedes both and covers all the same landmarks in one continuous run.

> ⚠️ `Congress Well Park` (map) and `Congress Bhavan` (handwritten note) may be two different locations — confirm which is intended.
